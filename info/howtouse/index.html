About

Satelitemap.space and associated IOS App is a solo developer project that has been on the web from before AI slop was even a term. The core visualization and calculation engine is not vibe coded. Unfortunately I know every function and every line of javascript, for better or worse.

This is not a resource affiliated in any way with SpaceX or Starlink internet. The data comes from space-track.org and other open data sources. The calculations and visualizations are best effort (for science education and casual viewing).

How to Use

satellitemap.space is desigend to allow everyone to explore the world of orbiting satellites and satellite constellations. To go to the main visualizer click on the logo in the top left.

Login/Logout

  • Use the More menu to create an account, /login or /logout, as required.

Preferences

  • Settings, Preferences, Satellite bookmarks and so on are stored per browser, or per logged-in account depending on which is appropriate.

Controls

Mouse: Click to drag to move globe around. Click and drag on a satellite to move the visualization intuitively. Long click to switch into no-distraction mode. Zoom in or out using scroll wheel or two finger touch pad zoom guestures.

Mobile: Use familiar touch operations to move and zoom/unzoom globe. Long touch to go to no-distraction mode.

Tips

  • When clicking on a satellite, it is added to a selector widget. Click on the i button in that widget to get more details on that satellite. The selector box can be dragged around the screen to move it where you prefer.
  • There are several basic view modes: Orbital, Home location or Satellite close view, landing at home (ground view), and POV satellite.
  • Pressing the Home button at the bottom moves camera to look at your home location. Pressing it again goes to ground level. Opening a satellite information panel reveals more options includong one to ride on the satellite.
  • When riding on a satellite toggle between look directions and camera modes but the camera is movable in all of them. Zooming in out changes field of view: /vis/satellite/norad/25544/pov
  • Rapidly flashing red satellites are re-entering shortly. Solid red satellites have re-entered in the last day and they stay static at their re-entry location. There is a full re-entry information page at /re-entries
  • Expore the navigation Functions menus: calculate sky-transits, look for satellite-celestial transits ( /transit-finder ), find recently launched sky-trains ( /vis/trains ) and more.
  • Use the Functions .. Visualizer .. Clear and Load functions to load satellites with various filters. Use Share to snapshot the current state and get a re-usable URL.
  • The legend box at the lower right offers a number of Filters which colorize satellites in various ways. Click on colors to de-emphasize that category.
  • Going to Home location while viewing Starlink constellation shows an updated list of local starlink satellites that may be transmitting to your router.
  • The Layers icon allows toggling on and off data layers on the globe such as ground stations, launch facilities, recent re-entries and more
  • Earth observation satellites such as the /vis/constellation/planet Planet series render with a different footprint style than Internet satellites!
  • Manipulate the clock and date to recover satellite positions at a past moment in time.
  • Reproduce and identify satellite streaks in photos and see movements across different camera configurations (location, focal length and so on), or in a deep space photo using /satellite-photobomb
  • Use the extensive Amateur Satellite /aamateur-radio page to listen to satellites.
